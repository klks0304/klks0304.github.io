🚀 1in746 — The Hidden Figures

From Launch to Return · Engineering Trade-offs with Real NASA Data

What is this?

This is a comprehensive space mission simulation from launch to return to Earth. Balance life support, power, radiation shielding, and food while facing real NASA data and events.

Be one in 8 billion — experience the engineering trade-offs and experiments that determine mission success or failure.

Why “1 in 746”?
Only about 746 humans have ever reached space. Being an astronaut is extremely rare — roughly 1 in 8 billion. This simulation lets you experience the challenges and decisions that come with that rare opportunity.

The number keeps growing as new astronauts, like future you, reach space...
