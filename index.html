🚀 1in746 — Be One of the Astronauts

Comprehensive Space Mission Simulation · Test your decision-making under pressure

What is this?

This is a comprehensive space mission simulation. The system randomly assigns your profession, landing site, and the events you will encounter.

The goal is not a knowledge quiz. It is to test how you, as a potential astronaut, make engineering trade-offs and team decisions when resources are limited and information is incomplete.

Why “1 in 746”?
As of now, only about 746 humans have ever left Earth’s atmosphere and reached space. Becoming an astronaut is extremely rare. This simulation lets you experience the challenges and choices that come with that rare role.
