What is this?

This is a comprehensive space mission simulation from launch to return to Earth. You will balance life support, power, radiation shielding, and food while making engineering decisions under uncertainty.

Why “1 in 746”?
Only about 746 humans have ever reached space. This simulation lets you experience the trade-offs that decide mission success or failure.
